I've put mockups for the plan on one canvas in `Nurulloxon Mockups.dc.html`. They're still being checked.

- **1a:** the full 8-section portfolio at 390px, in Uzbek with the correct ʻ character.
- **1b:** the hero in dark mode, in Russian, to check the Cyrillic.
- **1c:** the desktop hero, with SAT Math 750 shown as a point on a 200–800 scale.
- **1d–1g:** the diagnostic question and results screens, then the mistake analyzer's input and AI result screens. The result screen includes the "may be wrong" warning and the "AI can make mistakes" note.
- **1h:** the teacher dashboard, showing the class code, the student list, the class's most-missed skills and flagged explanations.

Things to know:
- **Fonts and colours:** these follow the project's attached design system, which uses a serif pair (Cormorant Garamond + Lora). Both fonts cover Cyrillic and ʻ. That replaces the plan's Unbounded / Golos / JetBrains Mono suggestion, and the plan's "avoid cream-and-terracotta" rule sits close to this system's warm palette. The graph-paper background, plotted curve and axis ticks from the plan are kept.
- **No invented facts:** every missing fact shows as a dashed `TODO:` chip. The platform screens are marked "Namuna maʼlumot" (sample data).
- **Still placeholders:** the AI explanation text is "…", and the QR code is a stand-in pattern.
